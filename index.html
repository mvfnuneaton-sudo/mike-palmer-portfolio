I'm the person who asks “Why are we doing it this way?” — then works with the people involved to find a better answer.

My career has moved between digital trading, ecommerce management, marketplace leadership, key accounts and business systems. I've owned the sales number, the customer journey, the product range and the fulfilment behind it — and I've also worked out how to connect the technology that supports it.

That combination is what I enjoy most: seeing both the commercial opportunity and the operational friction. Whether I'm selecting a platform, improving conversion, fixing an EDI feed or building a member app, I start with the people using it, then design and deliver a practical change.
