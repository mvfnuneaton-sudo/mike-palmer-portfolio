I'm the person who asks “Why are we doing it this way?” — then works with the people involved to find a better answer.

My career has moved between ecommerce, marketplaces, key accounts, digital trading and systems leadership. I’ve dealt with the commercial pressure of trading, the operational reality of orders and stock, and the technical detail of connecting the systems behind it all.

01FFN APPCOMMUNITY · OPERATIONS

One club.
One connected system.

Availability lived in one system. Payments were separate. Stats were kept in spreadsheets. It worked, but it was disjointed for players and organisers.

THE PROBLEM

Repeated admin, disconnected information and too many places to manage the same player journey.

THE RESPONSE

Design a joined-up, mobile-first experience around how players and club administrators really work.

The result is a single platform for matchday availability, balances, fitness progress, statistics and club management — evolving with feedback from the people using it.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Planned the player and admin journeys, unified fragmented processes, designed mobile interfaces and iterated the system through live usage.
